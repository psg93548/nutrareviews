## Key takeaways
- Exact ingredient forms and doses matter more than a long ingredient list.

## Why nutra reviews matter in 2026
Quality in supplements is mostly invisible: it lives in the sourcing, the testing and the label. A shorter, transparent formula usually beats one padded with token inclusions, and normalising to a per-serving basis reveals the true cost.
